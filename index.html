Tong Wu

Associate Professor of Practice
Department of Mathematics
University of Texas at San Antonio

I am an applied mathematician whose research focuses on developing numerical methods for solving nonlinear partial differential equations, continuous data assimilation algorithms, and computational epidemiology using network-based models.

My work spans finite-volume and moving-mesh schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws (including the shallow water equations), data assimilation for PDE-based forecast models, and agent-based network generation and spatial modeling for infectious diseases such as mosquito-borne illnesses.
